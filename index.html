PMR Groups · Refrigeration

PMR Groups works in industrial refrigeration through P.M. Refrigeration Pvt. Ltd. We supply refrigeration components, controls, compressors, spares and refrigerant gases for ammonia and Freon systems, and support customers with the technical work around them.

The head office is in Kilpauk, Chennai, with a branch office at Bhimmavaram, Andhra Pradesh.

Our journey

The PMR business has worked in industrial refrigeration for more than two decades. The current company, P.M. Refrigeration Private Limited, was incorporated in 2017 and continues that work.

A word from our CEO

"PMR has always been built around relationships."

When I look at PMR's journey, what I value most is not how much the business has grown. It is the relationships we have built along the way.

For more than 20 years we have worked with refrigeration contractors, plant operators and engineers across India. Many of them have been with us for a long time. What we do

We source refrigeration components, check they suit the plant, deliver them and support them after installation.

  1. 01

    Source

    Valves, controls, compressors, drives, spares and refrigerant gases from Danfoss, Emerson, Bock and SPI.

  2. 02

    Select

    We match each part to the refrigerant, the duty and the line size before it is ordered.

  3. 03

    Supply

    Stock is held at the Chennai stores. Orders are packed and despatched to site.

  4. 04

    Support

    Installation and commissioning help, troubleshooting, site training and tele-support after the part arrives.
